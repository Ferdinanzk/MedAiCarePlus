# Medicine intake inside a live chat: how it works today and what the request needs (18 October 2026)

The user's request (18 Oct): during a medicine intake, use face recognition, pill-intake recognition and emotion
recognition; if the robot's frames are not enough, stream them to the laptop for detection; and keep sending the
video after the intake. This map comes from a read-only workflow (`wf_9efceda2-ebf`: four mappers and a synthesis
that checked the numbers itself) over the server (`tool-fixed-version-03`, code `8826c92`), robot app 0.16.0
(`c56bbe2`) and the logs and database of the 16:55 test. **Nothing here is built yet.** Times are Taipei time.

## How it works today

The 16:55 intake was decided on the patient's word alone. The face check never recognised the patient during the watch, so the pill detector and emotion never ran. The camera was sending 2 frames a second. Here is each step, in Taipei time; I checked every number below myself.

1. The chat. The test ran inside a check-in task with no doses. The journal shows "task 184009b4-... (checkin): 0 dose(s)" at 16:51:19 and "SEARCHING -> CHECKIN" at 16:51:48.8. The robot never entered its dose flow: there is no WATCHING line. When it enters a chat, the robot caps the camera stream at COMPANION_CAPTURE_FPS = 2.0 (medcare-reachy-bridge session.py:61 and :568; runner.py:189-194). Before the chat, while searching, the server got 240 frames in 24 s, about 10 a second. That is the camera's ceiling: the journal at 16:51:34.9 says "the camera hands this app only 10.0 fps; the server records doses only at >= 12 fps".

2. The laptop already does all the detection. Every frame goes to /api/device/monitor/frame. There the laptop computes landmarks and runs face recognition (OpenVINO, the check-in rule openvino_only), the pill-intake detector and face emotion (FER+) (MedAiCarePlus api_device.py:893-945, monitor_service.py:515-560 and :596-625). So "stream it to the laptop" already happens; only the rate is low.

3. The patient asked. At 16:55:24.3 the patient said 「我現在要吃 second med」. GPT-Live called check_medication_due (server log 16:55:25.078), then start_pill_intake_detection (16:55:26.539, "ok in 6 ms"). That tool only stores a watch window in server memory, 30 s by default (tool_registry.py:52 and :485-507). It sends nothing to the robot and does not change the frame rate. Its own answer says "the chat's camera runs at 2 frames a second".

4. During the watch, the server got 61 frames from 16:55:26.5 to 16:55:57.1, which is 2 a second. There are 9 identity lines (server-1630-1710.log lines 1014-1077). Six say "status=mismatch rule=openvino_only ... ov_faces=1": one face was found, but it was not matched to the patient. Three say "status=searching": no face was found. None says verified. The patient was last verified at 16:51:46.491 (distance 0.227, 81 px) and next at 17:09:36.808. The detector only scores frames of a verified patient (monitor_service.py:520-528). Emotion is likewise scored only on a verified face. So neither ran at all.

5. The result. At 16:55:54.4 the patient said 「我吃完了」. GPT-Live called get_pill_intake_result at 16:55:55.417 and record_medication_taken, with the patient's word, at 16:55:57.120. The record read the watch again. Its stored evidence (dose_confirmation 5232a05d) is: detector_result not_taken, detector_state timeout, confidence 0.0, empty detector_evidence, landmark_fps 2.0, degraded true, emotion null, verification null. It was filed as a patient_claim family request (tool_registry.py:682-746), and intake 66 is now pending_confirmation. Nothing was recorded as taken. There is no dose_emotion row for intake 66, and no label check (verify_medication) ran.

6. Family. The request reached LINE at 16:55:59.88 (outbox 58). The clip 027c5c38 has 40 frames over 19.6 s, about 2 a second. It is the last 20 s before the record, about 16:55:37 to 16:55:57 (dose_video.py:48 and :123-133). It was made at 16:55:58.23 and logged at 16:55:58.246 as "kept unsent: no public link (tunnel not answering)". The link answered again at 17:03:47.3. The clip was "sent late" at 17:05:28.9 and LINE accepted it at 17:05:30.7 (outbox 59), 9.5 minutes after the request. The first 10.6 s of the watch are not in the clip, and nothing after the record is.

7. After the intake, the stream kept going to the laptop at 2 frames a second: 60 frames in the next 30 s, and 1768 until the camera session ended at 17:10:41.0. None of them was used for family. From 16:56:33 to 17:09:35 the robot logged 84 times that the patient's voice did not restart GPT-Live because the camera had not seen them lately.

8. The robot's own emotion scoring runs only on a face the server has verified, so it did nothing during the intake. Its only fallback line, at 17:09:38.9, says "scored 0 of 1 due frames ... scoring on the laptop". That was a false alarm on the first verified face after 18 minutes (governor.py:51-56 and :116-123; runner.py:399-408).

9. auto_record. The robot was re-paired at 16:50:04. The active device row 78d8a858 has auto_record FALSE, the column default. Only the two revoked rows have TRUE. Apart from that, a chat watch is an observe session, and an observe session can never record a dose by itself (monitor_service.py:345-346 and :421-422).

## Options

- **1. Speed up the chat stream to the camera's full rate during a watch** (medium; Both: robot (live.py, session.py, runner.py) and server (agent_bridge.py, tool_registry.py).): Add a new message from the server to the robot, such as camera_rate with an fps value and a time limit. The robot answers it the way it already answers the 'camera' request (agent_bridge.py:80). start_pill_intake_detection sends it, and the server sends a restore when the watch settles or closes. On the robot, live.py _control handles it and calls MonitorStream.set_max_fps(None) through a hook in SlotSession (the same pattern as chat.gestures and chat.mood, session.py:645-647). A robot-side timer goes back to COMPANION_CAPTURE_FPS: the watch's timeout plus a margin, at most about 150 s. The existing resets at chat end (session.py:913 and :995) stay. This gives 10 frames a second today, five times what the detector and the clip get now. Risks: Nobody has measured the CPU, Wi-Fi and speaker load at 10 frames a second with live audio running. The estimate is about 1.2 Mbit/s and about 12% of one core, and the chat already logged 3 speaker underruns at 2 frames a second (journal 17:10:40.069). An older robot app ignores the message (live.py:539-540), so the server must treat no answer as 'not raised'. Below 12 frames a second the session stays degraded, so the detector stays supporting evidence. It does nothing while the face is not verified, which was today's failure. It needs a robot app release and a deploy.
- **2. Help the face check during a watch** (small; Server for the answer, the prompt and the log line. Robot for the follower.): start_pill_intake_detection and get_pill_intake_result should say plainly when the patient is not recognised (the watch result already carries 'identity', tool_registry.py:549-556). Add a line to BACKEND_INSTRUCTION (gpt_live_config.py:101-117) so GPT-Live asks the patient to face Reachy and come closer before and during the watch. Log the best OpenVINO distance on mismatch lines, so the cause of today's 18-minute mismatch can be found. Later, run the face follower during a chat watch; it is part of the face lock already approved for robot app 0.17.0. Risks: The follower needs a verified face first, because it follows the server's target_box. So it cannot fix a mismatch by itself. Asking the patient to come closer adds talking during the intake. The cause of today's mismatch is still unknown.
- **3. Score emotion like a dose during a chat watch** (medium; Server (monitor_service.py, dose_emotion.py, tool_registry.py).): When the watch names a dose (medication_name), score emotion at the dose rate once the face is verified (DOSE_VISION_INTERVAL 0.25 s, monitor_service.py:362-368). Write a dose_emotion row for that dose and put the watch's emotion summary into the request's evidence. Also fix the request's wording: a record on the detector alone is always filed as 'degraded' (tool_registry.py:740), and its family text says the stream was not smooth even when it was. Risks: dose_emotion's write paths assume a dose session with intk_id (dose_emotion.py:305-306 and :330-340). They must change without changing the robot's own dose flow. Identity must stay at most twice a second. This only helps once the face is verified.
- **4. Raise the robot daemon's camera limit to 15 fps** (small; Robot (operations on the daemon).): Run tools/deploy_to_robot.py --camera-ipc-fps 15. It edits the daemon's media_server.py and keeps a backup. Then restart the daemon by hand. Risks: This crosses the 12 fps gate. Robot dose sessions would no longer be degraded. With auto_record TRUE, a policy-confirmed event would then record the dose as taken by itself, with no pill identification and no family request (api_device.py:779-781 and :817; monitor_service.py:690-700). That is decision D2. The CPU load at 15 frames a second with live audio is unmeasured, and any daemon update undoes the change. On its own it does nothing for the chat, which stays at 2 frames a second until option 1 exists.
- **5. Hand the intake over to the robot's own dose flow** (large; Both.): When the patient says in a chat that they will take a medicine, end the chat (like the existing 'medicine' ending) and run the robot's dose flow for that dose (MED_PROMPT, then WATCHING). That flow has the full camera rate, a dose session, emotion at the dose rate, a dose_emotion row and the face follower. Risks: It ends the GPT-Live conversation. The dose must be startable now, and today's dose was 1.5 h before its 18:27 time. A dose session uses the strict with_mediapipe face rule, which fails beyond about 1.35 m (CLAUDE.md). It also puts the intake under auto_record and decision D2.
- **6. Put the moments after the intake into the family clip** (medium; Server (dose_video.py, tool_registry.py).): In dose_video.capture, take the frames from before the record at once, wait N seconds, add the newer buffered frames, then encode and send. Also start the window at the watch's start, so a 30 s watch is not cut down to its last 20 s (today 10.6 s were lost). Risks: The video notice in both languages promises 'the moments before it was recorded' and a 20 s buffer (en.json:16 and :34, zh-TW.json the same). So this needs a new notice and a TERMS_VERSION bump, and everyone must consent again (consent_service.py:54-56). The clip also reaches family N seconds later.
- **7. Make every clip reach family after a link outage** (small; Server.): Keep review, event and camera-recorded clips unsent instead of deleting them. Give retry_unsent a path for clips without an open request. Tell family in the request when the video will be late. Fix the 'the camera had no frames' wording, which gives the wrong reason (dose_event.py:107-109 and :139-143; taken_confirmation_job.py:153-162). Keeping the LINE tunnel up removes most late clips. Risks: Sending a clip after its request has closed is a product decision: round 3 of 17 Oct forbade sending old footage under a new request.
- **8. Fix the robot's emotion false alarm** (small; Robot (multimodal/governor.py).): Reset the governor's counters when more than WINDOW (10 s) passes with no due frame, so the first verified face after a long gap does not count as 'can't keep up' (governor.py:51-56 and :116-123). Risks: Low. Today it costs 60 s of robot-side scoring at the moment the patient comes back into view.

## Recommendation (Claude's, not yet approved)

Build one change, the 'chat watch', in three steps, and keep the daemon at 10 fps and auto_record FALSE until decision D2 is made.

Step 1 is option 2: help the face check. Today's failure was the face check, not the frame rate. With no verified face, the detector and emotion do not run at any frame rate.

Step 2 is option 1. The server asks the robot for the camera's full rate (10 frames a second today) for the length of the watch, and the robot has its own timer back to 2. Before shipping it, measure CPU, Wi-Fi and speaker underruns on the Pi with GPT-Live running.

Step 3 is option 3: emotion at the dose rate and a dose_emotion row for the watched dose.

The chat watch stays an observe session, so it can never record a dose by itself, and every result still goes to family as a confirmation request. The frames already go to the laptop, so this is all 'stream it to the laptop' needs.

Do the video part (option 6 or 7) only after the user says which meaning they want. Do not do option 4 (15 fps) or option 5 now. Option 8 is a small fix that can go in the same robot release.

## Safety first

- auto_record: the brief says it is TRUE on both rows, but reachy_device now has three rows. The active device 78d8a858 (created 16:50:04 by today's re-pairing) has auto_record FALSE, the column default. Only the revoked rows 04816ab9 and f17e0632 are TRUE. Re-pairing changed the setting without anyone deciding it. Confirm FALSE is what the user wants, keep it FALSE until D2 (CLAUDE.md:78), and check it again after every re-pair.
- The 12 fps gate is the only thing that today stops a robot dose session with auto_record TRUE from recording a dose by itself without identifying the pill (monitor_service.py:29 and :343-350; api_device.py:817). At IPC_FPS 10 every session is degraded. Do not raise IPC_FPS to 15 before D2 is decided and auto_record is confirmed FALSE (docs/TOOL_PRIORITIES_2026-10-18.md:27 and :67 already say this).
- Keep a chat watch an observe session. Observe sessions force auto_commit off and never make a candidate (monitor_service.py:345-346 and :421-422), and record_medication_taken always files a family request (tool_registry.py:744-746). Switching the chat watch to a dose session (option 5) would put it under auto_record and D2.
- A record on the camera alone: in observe mode, a 'confirmed' event is not downgraded on a slow stream, so record_medication_taken can accept it without the patient's word (tool_registry.py:682-694), filed as 'degraded'. A faster stream makes this more likely. It still only asks family, but the user should decide whether it is allowed.
- A raised stream must come back down by itself: a robot-side time limit plus the existing resets at chat end (session.py:913 and :995). Then a lost 'restore' message never leaves the camera at 10 frames a second for a whole chat.
- Measure the Pi at 10 frames a second with GPT-Live audio running (CPU, Wi-Fi, speaker underruns) before shipping. It has never been measured, and there were already 3 underruns at 2 frames a second (journal 17:10:40.069).
- Consent: a faster stream does not change what is saved. But moments after the intake in the clip would break the video notice (en.json:16 and :34) and need a new TERMS_VERSION and consent again. Also, frames are already buffered for the clip during the whole chat (api_device.py:824 and :920-921), while the notice says 'during medication time'.
- A side finding, not about the frame rate: the server's access log writes each family member's private video-link token in plain text. There are 4 'GET /api/media/line/' lines in server-1630-1710.log, and I did not print the tokens. The design says only a SHA-256 of the token is kept (dose_video.py:404-405 and :438-439). Three of the 4 lines are family video links; the one at 17:03:46.9 is the server's own link probe. (The session's log copies were cleaned of the tokens.)

## Not verified

- Why OpenVINO answered 'mismatch' (one face, not matched to the patient) for 18 minutes, when the same person verified at 16:51:46 and 17:09:36. Possible causes are angle, distance, light, a hand or the medicine in front of the face, or another person. No frames are kept, and the mismatch lines log no distance, so only a live check can tell.
- The real size of a streamed JPEG. The figure of about 15 KB is an estimate from the clip's preview frame re-encoded at quality 70.
- The CPU, Wi-Fi and speaker load at 10-15 frames a second while live audio runs. Nobody has measured it.
- Whether 10 frames a second is enough for the detector inside a chat. The only evidence at 10 comes from the robot's dose flow, where it was degraded but usable.
- The timeout_seconds GPT-Live passed and the answer of the standalone get_pill_intake_result at 16:55:55.4. Neither is logged. The stored 'timeout' reading came from the record's own read at 16:55:57.1, 30.6 s after the watch opened, so the default of 30 s fits. One mapper's inference that the timeout was under 29 s is not supported by the logs.
- Why today's earlier robot dose clips (intakes 122 and 123) are about 6.5 frames a second, not 10. The likely cause is the clip buffer's rule that drops a frame arriving less than 0.1 s after the last kept one (dose_video.py:43 and :111-112). Replaying that rule on the server log times of the 10-a-second search (16:51:25-16:51:45) keeps 115 of 200 frames, about 5.8 a second. Log times are not exact arrival times, so this is likely, not proven.
- Whether Reachy's downcast1 head move (about 16:55:33-16:55:40) caused the one zero-face 'searching' line at 16:55:35.3. It is a correlation only.
- What caused the LINE tunnel outage from 16:47 to 17:03. The tunnel container was created at 17:03:12.
- The robot-to-laptop clock offset. The mappers found 0.9 s and 1.24 s. It does not change any conclusion here.
