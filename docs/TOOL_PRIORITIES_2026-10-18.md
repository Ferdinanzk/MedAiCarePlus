**Pass or fail:** A step passes only when its tool shows in the server log (`live agent tool <name>`, `[TOOL]`, or the relay's backstop line) AND its own check holds: the SQL row, the robot journal line, or the clock or weather site. Never pass a step on the "ok" line alone (agent_bridge.py:253-254 logs ok whenever the call returned). A step whose words the camera gate refused is BLOCKED; repeat it up to 2 times. The run PASSES when steps 2 and 5-15 pass. It is PARTIAL when only blocked steps are left, or when a tool took the other allowed path (cancel all instead of by id, the antenna emote instead of play_emotion, or the backstop instead of get_pill_intake_result). It FAILS when a tool is never called after 2 tries, or when a check contradicts the log. The intake reading (taken, uncertain, not_taken or still_monitoring, with its confidence and fps) is something to write down, not a pass or fail item.

**Watch out**
- **Afterwards:** archive 測試藥 within 2 h, so the expiry sends no missed-dose alert. Then list the waiting family messages (`SELECT outbox_id, kind, status, last_error FROM notification_outbox WHERE status IN ('queued','failed');`) and clear them with #3 before LINE goes back on, or they all go out at once.
- **Risk words:** don't say fall, pain or self-harm words in this run (for example 「我跌倒了」 or 「我胸口痛」). With LINE off, an alert reaches nobody, and the chat would end flagged, with no summary.
- **Camera gate:** after 30 s without your words, GPT-Live closes, and your next words must pass the 15 s camera gate again. The dance also turns the camera away. If Reachy is silent for 3 s, lean in and look at it.
- **The robot's own dose task:** if steps 1-5 are not finished in time, the chat ends and the robot runs its own dose flow at 10 fps.
- **Intake reading:** the detector is tuned for about 15 fps and the chat sends 2 fps, so "uncertain" or "not_taken" is expected. Family requests quote that reading, which is why we measure it.
- **Cloud vision:** with LIVE_VISION_CLOUD=1, step 2 may send camera frames to Gemini. This is for your own tests only.
- **Old model:** if you run the test on or after 20 Oct before #4 is done, watch for "gave no usable answer" warnings from the risk check.
